# The Wedding Film — Muniza & Muzamil 💍✨

A luxury editorial wedding platform, cinematic film experience, and private living heirloom created for **Muniza** & **Muzamil**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🎬 1. Public Wedding Film Experience
- **Cinematic Prologue & Sound Activation**: Atmospheric movie-like opening titles.
- **Hero Typography & Parallax**: Editorial font pairing (*Cinzel*, *Cormorant Garamond*, *Alex Brush*).
- **Interactive Story Timeline**: Scroll-driven liquid gold line drawing, glowing emitter, and chapter milestones.
- **Tactile 3D Royal Invitation**: Perspective mouse tracking, dynamic gold foil sheen, and wax seal unsealing with crystal acoustic chimes.
- **Events Itinerary**: Mehndi, Baraat, Walima with Google Maps and 1-click `.ics` calendar exports.
- **4K Wedding Film Player**: Cinema viewer with ambient lighting.
- **Curated Masonry Gallery**: Full-screen editorial lightbox.
- **Voice Guestbook**: Browser microphone audio recorder + acoustic waveform player.
- **Memory Wall**: Guest photo uploads with printable wedding table QR code.
- **Conversational RSVP**: Step-by-step guest RSVP with confetti celebration.

### ✦ 2. "Our Little Universe" (Private Couple Sanctuary)
A deeply emotional, private digital world for the bride and groom:
- **Two Hearts — Two Stories**: Her Story ❤️ vs His Story 🤍 with diary perspective flips and "One Story" merge.
- **Why I Chose You**: Step-by-step reasons + *"And I would choose you again."*
- **Secret Letters**: Time-locked letters with authentic paper unfolding.
- **Open When...**: 7+ sealed situational envelopes with custom creation.
- **Future Us**: 5 sacred wedding day questions locked for 1, 5, and 10-year milestones.
- **When We’re Old**: 2046 locked heirloom box (*“Open when you’ve been married for 20 years”*).
- **Our Firsts**: 12-milestone horizontal ribbon timeline.
- **One Photo — Two Memories**: Dual perspective photo storytelling.
- **24-Hour Wedding Day Chronicle**: From morning mist to Rukhsati, automatically becoming *"The Day We Became Us"*.
- **Family Blessings**: Words from Her Family, His Family, Friends, and Elders with acoustic voice waveforms.
- **The Final Outro Signature**: *"US... Forever starts with a day. But our story doesn't end there."*

### 🛠️ 3. Couple Studio & Life-Cycle System
- Live timeline switcher: *Before Wedding*, *Wedding Day*, *After Wedding*, and *Anniversary Mode*.
- RSVP analytics, guest manager, and theme engine.
